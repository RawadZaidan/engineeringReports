What This SOP Covers

This document defines the complete lifecycle of a maintenance request in the MEDILab Asset Management platform — from the moment a client submits a request, through engineer assignment and scheduling, to on-site service execution and finalisation of a signed service report with billing classification. It applies to all corrective maintenance, preventive maintenance, repair, and installation jobs handled by the MEDILab engineering team.

All engineers and the Engineering Manager must follow this procedure for every maintenance request. No service visit may be executed without an assigned, scheduled request in the system. No job is considered closed until a saved and client-signed service report exists in the platform.

00Request Lifecycle Overview

Every maintenance request passes through the following statuses. Each status transition requires a specific action. The system does not advance a request automatically — each step must be completed by the responsible role.

01Purpose

This SOP establishes the standardised procedure for managing medical equipment maintenance requests from intake to closure at MEDILab S.A.L. It defines the responsibilities of the Engineering Manager and field engineers, the required steps for scheduling service visits, and the mandatory format and content of the service report that must be completed at the end of every job.

Following this procedure consistently ensures accurate job tracking, client accountability (via signed reports), correct billing classification, and a complete audit trail for all equipment serviced under MEDILab's remit.

02Scope

Who this applies to: All MEDILab field engineers, and the Engineering Manager responsible for assigning and overseeing maintenance requests.

What is covered: All maintenance requests submitted via the Asset Management v2.0 platform — including corrective repair, preventive maintenance (PM), installation, commissioning, and training visits.

What is NOT covered: Procurement of spare parts (see MED-PROC-001); fleet and transport logistics (see MED-LOG-001); warranty claims management with manufacturers; third-party contractor visits not managed through the MEDILab platform.

⚠️
Scheduling Window: The system enforces a valid scheduling range per request. Engineers must be assigned within the displayed valid date window (e.g. "Valid Range: 21 May 2026 – 28 May 2026"). Do not schedule visits outside this window without explicit client confirmation and Engineering Manager approval.

04Roles & Responsibilities
RoleResponsibility in This Procedure
Engineering ManagerReviews all incoming open requests on the dashboard. Assigns the appropriate engineer and confirms date/time window. Adds notes/instructions for the engineer prior to visit. Reviews billing classification after visit and updates engineer's quote/valuation for billable visits.
EngineerExecutes the scheduled service visit at the client site. Creates, fills, and saves the service report on the platform immediately after the visit. Obtains the client representative's signature on the report. Completes all four service report sections accurately.
Client RepresentativeSigns the service report to confirm the work was performed and received. Primary contact for logistics at the client site.

05Prerequisites
  • Engineering Manager has active login to Asset Management v2.0 with admin/manager permissions.
  • Assigned engineer has active login to Asset Management v2.0 and can access the request and service report forms.
  • The maintenance request has been submitted by the client or internal requester and has status Open (Requires Scheduling).
  • The equipment to be serviced exists in the MEDILab asset registry with a valid registry entry (model and S/N).
  • The engineer has been briefed on any special materials or tools required (e.g. calibration solutions, spare parts) — per the Notes/Instructions field.
  • For billable visits: the Engineering Manager has verified the client's billing agreement type (Billable / Contract / Warranty) before the visit.

Dashboard layout: Filter tabs — All / Open / Scheduled / Active / Closed. Use the For Me toggle to narrow to your assigned requests. Use Sort by Urgency to surface critical jobs.

Key fields to note on each card: MR number, facility name, location, requested by, due date, and urgency badge.

PHASE BENGINEER — VISIT EXECUTION & SERVICE REPORT INITIATION
Purpose of this phase The assigned engineer travels to the client site, performs the service, and immediately initiates and saves the service report in the platform upon completion. The service report must be created on the same day as the visit wherever possible.
5
Review the scheduled request details and Notes/Instructions before departing for the visit. Engineer
Log in to Asset Management v2.0 and locate your assigned request (status: Scheduled). Read the service information, equipment details, facility location, client contact, and the Engineering Manager's notes. Confirm you have all required tools and materials before departing.

Check: correct facility address, client representative name and phone number, equipment model and S/N to expect on site, and any special instructions from the Engineering Manager.

6
Execute the service visit at the client site and note all findings and actions taken. Engineer
Perform the required maintenance, repair, calibration, or other service. Document as you go: the issue found, the work performed, any parts used or replaced, and the final equipment status. You will enter this information in the service report in the next step.
⚠️
Parts Used: Record every part used — part name, quantity, and reference where possible. If no parts were used, enter "N/A". Do not leave this field blank.
7
Open the request in the platform and click "Create Service Report." Engineer
Navigate to the maintenance request (MR-XXX) for this visit. From the request detail view, click the green Create Service Report button. This opens the four-section service report form. The system auto-saves periodically — you will see "Last auto-saved: Just now" at the top.
PHASE CENGINEER — SERVICE REPORT COMPLETION (4 SECTIONS)
Purpose of this phase The engineer fills in all four sections of the service report accurately and completely. All sections are mandatory. The report cannot be considered complete unless all four sections are filled and the client signature is captured.
8
Complete Section 1: General Information. Engineer
Fill in or verify all fields in Section 1. Most fields are pre-populated from the linked request — review and correct any that are wrong.

Fields in Section 1:

  • Linked Request: Auto-populated from the parent MR — verify the correct MR number is shown (e.g. MR-164 | AUB-OSB).
  • Client Name: The facility/organisation name (e.g. AUB-OSB). Verify it matches the actual client.
  • Project Reference: Enter or confirm the project code (e.g. UIU9998). Obtain from the Engineering Manager if unknown.
  • Location / Department: City and/or department where the service was performed (e.g. Beirut).
  • Donor (if applicable): If equipment or project is funded by a donor (e.g. USAID), enter the donor name. Leave blank if not applicable.
  • Date of Service: Set to the actual date and time the service was performed. Do not use a future date.
  • Client Representative: Full name of the person who received the service at the facility.
  • Representative Phone Number: Contact number of the client representative.

10
Complete Section 3: Service Details. Engineer
This is the core technical section of the report. Every field must be completed accurately and professionally. Use clear, precise language — the service report is a legal and billing document.

Fields in Section 3:

  • Issue Description: Describe the problem reported by the client or found on arrival. Be specific (e.g. "Biowetec chemistry analyzer produces out-of-range values during calibration"). Avoid vague terms.
  • Work Performed: Describe all actions taken during the visit. Include specific procedures, tests, and outcomes (e.g. "Cleaned reagent probe, ran calibration test sequence, verified result consistency across 3 reagent cycles").
  • Parts Used: List all parts used or replaced. If none: enter "N/A". Never leave blank.
  • Status: Select the applicable status — Completed, Pending, or Requires Follow-up.
  • Follow-up Required?: Check this box if the job requires a second visit or further action.

Service Type checkboxes (select all that apply): Preventive Maintenance, Repair, Training, Installation, Commissioning.

Billing/Contract Category (select one): Paid Service, Contract, Warranty, Other.

Final Equipment Status After Service (select one): Returned to working conditions, Needs Follow Up, Collected for maintenance.

Click "Save Report" to finalise and submit the service report. Engineer

PHASE DENGINEERING MANAGER — BILLING REVIEW & CLOSURE
Purpose of this phase After the engineer saves the service report, the Engineering Manager reviews the billing classification and updates the financial valuation for any billable visits. This phase closes the request and ensures the finance team can proceed with invoicing.
14
Review the completed service report and verify billing classification. Engineering Manager
Open the relevant maintenance request and review the saved service report. Confirm the service type, billing/contract category, and final equipment status match expectations. Check whether the visit should generate an invoice.
⚡ Decision — Billing Type
BILLABLE →Engineer's quote is likely $0.00 (default). Proceed to Step 15 to update valuation.
CONTRACT / WARRANTY →No invoice required. Verify agreement type is correct in the system. Proceed to Step 16 (closure).
15
Click "Update Valuation" and enter the correct billing amount for billable visits. Engineering Manager
In the Billing & Warranty panel of the request, click Update Valuation. Enter the correct engineer's quote / service charge based on the work performed, parts used, and applicable pricing. Save the updated valuation. This figure will be used by the finance team for client invoicing.

Agreement type will display as Billable. The default $0.00 must be updated before the request is handed to finance. Confirm the final amount with the Operations Director if it exceeds the standard rate card.

08Non-Conformance & Exception Handling
🚫
No visit without a scheduled request: No engineer may perform a service visit at a client site without an existing maintenance request that has been formally scheduled in Asset Management v2.0. Visits performed without a scheduled MR cannot be invoiced and will not be covered by MEDILab liability. Report any such occurrence to the Engineering Manager immediately.
⚠️
Client representative unavailable for signature: If the client representative is unavailable to sign, the engineer must note this in the Work Performed field with the reason and time. The Engineering Manager must follow up with the client to obtain a signature within 48 hours. Do not leave signature blank without documentation.
⚠️
Equipment not in registry: If the equipment to be serviced is not found in the MEDILab asset registry (Section 2), do not create a placeholder entry. Stop, notify the Engineering Manager, and have the asset added to the registry before completing the report.
ℹ️
Visit cannot be completed (equipment issue): If the engineer arrives on site and cannot complete the service (e.g. equipment has been moved, access denied, parts unavailable), set the report Status to "Pending" and check "Follow-up Required." Document the reason in the Work Performed field. Notify the Engineering Manager before leaving the site.
ℹ️
Platform offline / cannot save report: If the platform is unavailable when the engineer needs to create the report, complete the service details on a paper template (or screenshot form fields on mobile). Submit the paper report to the Engineering Manager and create the digital report in the platform on the same day once connectivity is restored.
