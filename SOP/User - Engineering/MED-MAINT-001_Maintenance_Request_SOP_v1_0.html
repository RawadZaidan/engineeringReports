Before You Begin — Read This First

This SOP covers the full lifecycle of a Maintenance Request in the MEDILab platform: from opening the form and entering customer details, to selecting equipment, defining service type, setting billing status, and saving the request. It also describes what happens after submission and how requestors and the Sales/Engineering team track progress.

Every maintenance, installation, training, repair, or commissioning engagement with a client facility must have a corresponding Maintenance Request record in the system before any field visit occurs. Verbal authorizations are not sufficient — the request must exist in the platform.

01Purpose

This SOP establishes the standardized procedure for registering equipment maintenance, repair, installation, training, and commissioning requests in the MEDILab platform. It ensures all client-facing service engagements are formally documented before execution, linked to the correct equipment asset, properly classified by billing status, and visible to the scheduling and engineering teams.

Consistent use of this procedure ensures: complete service audit trails, accurate donor/project attribution, correct billing classification, and no field visits dispatched without a registered and traceable request.

02Scope

Who this applies to: MEDILab Sales staff, Clinical Engineers, and any team member registering a client service request. Applies to all facilities served by MEDILab across Lebanon and donor-funded projects.

What is covered: All service types — Preventive Maintenance, Training, Installation, Repair, and Commissioning — for any registered equipment in the MEDILab asset registry.

What is NOT covered: Engineer scheduling and field dispatch (see MED-MAINT-002); equipment registry management and new machine onboarding (see MED-ASSET-001); spare parts procurement workflows.

03Definitions & Abbreviations
Term / AbbreviationDefinition
Maintenance RequestA formal service engagement record created in the MEDILab platform before any field visit or service delivery. Tracks customer, facility, equipment, service type, billing, and scheduling details.
Availability WindowThe date range (Start → End) during which the client facility is available to receive the service. Used by the engineering team for scheduling.
Urgency LevelPriority classification for the request: High (critical equipment failure), Medium (standard service), Low (non-urgent). Affects scheduling priority.
Equipment RegistryThe master list of client-owned equipment tracked in the MEDILab platform, identified by Catalogue model and Serial Number (S/N).
Billing StatusDefines how the service will be charged: Under Warranty, Billable, Under Contract, or Free of Charge (FOC).
Service TypeThe category of service required. Multiple types may be selected per request: PM, Training, Installation, Repair, Commissioning.
Donor / ProjectThe funding source or project code if the equipment was supplied under a donor program (e.g. USAID, UNDP, WHO). Required for project-attributed services.
S/NSerial Number — unique identifier for a specific equipment unit in the registry.
PMPreventive Maintenance — scheduled routine service to ensure equipment performance and longevity.
FOCFree of Charge — service provided at no cost to the client (e.g. goodwill visit, donor-covered service).

PHASE BSECTION 1 — CUSTOMER & FACILITY DETAILS
Purpose of this phase Capture who the client is, where the equipment is located, when the contact was made, and who the technical contact is at the facility. This data drives traceability and client communication.
3
Enter the Customer Contact Date in the date field. Requestor
This is the date the client contacted MEDILab (or was contacted by MEDILab) to initiate the service request — not today's date unless the request is being registered on the same day as the contact. Use the calendar picker or type in MM/DD/YYYY format.

Example: If the client called on April 22, 2026 but you are registering today, enter 04/22/2026, not today's date. Accurate contact dates are critical for SLA tracking.

4
Enter the Facility Name exactly as registered in the platform. Requestor
Type the full facility name as it appears in MEDILab's client database. This is a free-text field — ensure spelling matches the registered client name to avoid duplicate records and mis-attribution.

Example: AUB - OSB (not "AUB" alone or "American University of Beirut"). When in doubt, check the equipment registry — the facility name used there is the canonical reference.

5
Select the Location (District) from the dropdown. Requestor
Choose the Lebanese district where the facility is physically located. This is used by the engineering team for route planning and field dispatch.
6
Enter the Donor / Project code if the equipment was supplied under a funded program. Requestor
If the equipment was procured through a donor project (e.g. USAID, UNDP, WHO), enter the project name or code. Leave blank for standard commercial clients.

Example: Enter USAID if the equipment was procured under a USAID supply contract. This field is critical for donor reporting and cost attribution.

7
Enter the Contact Person Name and Contact Phone Number. Requestor
Record the name and direct phone number of the person at the facility who will coordinate access for the field engineer. Use the format +961 XX XXX XXX for Lebanese numbers.

PHASE CSECTION 2 — SERVICE WINDOWS & PRIORITY
Purpose of this phase Define when the client is available to receive the service and how urgently it needs to occur. This drives the engineering team's scheduling queue.
8
Set the Availability Start and Availability End dates using the date pickers. Requestor
Enter the date range during which the facility and the relevant staff are available to receive the service. These dates come from the client — confirm them during the contact call before registering. The engineering team will schedule the visit within this window.

Minimum window: The availability window should be at least 3 business days to give the engineering team scheduling flexibility. A 1-day window on a high-urgency request may result in conflicts.

Example: Availability Start: 04/23/2026  |  Availability End: 04/30/2026

PHASE FSAVE & CONFIRM SUBMISSION
Purpose of this phase Submit the completed request to the system, confirm it was registered successfully, and verify it appears in the dashboard queue.
14
Review all four sections before clicking "Save Request". Requestor
Before saving, scroll through all sections and confirm: correct contact date, exact facility name, valid availability window, equipment correctly linked, service types selected, billing status verified. The "Save Request" button is located at the bottom-right of the form in red.
⚠️
Missing required fields: If any required field is blank, the platform will prevent saving and highlight the empty field in red. Complete all highlighted fields before re-attempting to save.
15
Click "Save Request" and wait for the system to finish processing. Requestor
After clicking Save, a loading screen will appear with the message "Processing Request… Please do not close this window." Wait for this to complete fully — do not navigate away or close the browser tab while processing is in progress.

Contact the facility's designated contact person (from Section 1) to confirm registration. Inform them of the expected scheduling timeline based on the urgency level selected. Do not commit to a specific visit date — this is determined by the engineering team after review.

08Non-Conformance & Exception Handling
⚠️
Equipment Not in Registry: If the equipment the client requires service for is not listed in the "Select machine from Registry" dropdown, do not proceed with a generic or placeholder entry. Click "Register New Machine" and follow MED-ASSET-001 to add the asset first. Only then return to register the maintenance request.
⚠️
Incorrect Billing Status Saved: If the wrong billing status was selected and the request has already been saved, do not delete and re-create the request. Contact the Clinical Engineering Manager to correct the billing classification before the request moves to "Scheduled" status. Corrections after scheduling require Operations Manager sign-off.
🚫
Field Visit Conducted Without a Registered Request: Any service visit executed without a corresponding Open or Scheduled request in the system is a non-conformance. The requestor or engineer must register a retroactive request immediately upon return and flag it to the Clinical Engineering Manager with a note in the Additional Details field: "RETROACTIVE — visit conducted on [date] due to [reason]."
ℹ️
Client Requests Emergency Same-Day Service: Same-day requests are permitted in emergency situations (critical equipment failure with patient care impact). Set Urgency to High, set Availability Start to today, and immediately notify the Clinical Engineering Manager by phone after saving — do not rely on dashboard monitoring alone for same-day critical requests.
