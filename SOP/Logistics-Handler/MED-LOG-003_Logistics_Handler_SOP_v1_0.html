About This Document

This SOP defines the end-to-end responsibilities of the Logistics Handler — the person accountable for reviewing, approving, editing, or denying trip requests submitted through MEDILab's Operational Logistics platform. It covers every action available from the Handler's dashboard and prescribes the exact communication required when a request is modified before approval.

Requestor-side instructions (how to submit a trip request) are covered separately in MED-LOG-001. Driver-side instructions are in MED-LOG-002. This document assumes the Logistics Handler has system access and at least one driver assigned to the fleet.

Read this document fully before processing any request. Every action you take in the system has downstream effects on drivers, requestors, and client-facing operations.

01Purpose
Purpose Statement This SOP establishes a standardised process for the Logistics Handler to review incoming trip requests, assess driver and vehicle availability, take the appropriate action (Approve, Edit & Approve, or Deny), and ensure requestors are proactively informed of any changes made to their submissions. The goal is to eliminate scheduling conflicts, prevent uninformed drivers from showing up with wrong instructions, and ensure requestors have a chance to validate trip details before a trip is locked.
02Scope

Applies to: Any person assigned the Logistics Handler role in the Operational Logistics platform. This includes HR Managers, Operations Coordinators, or any delegate temporarily performing this function.

Covers: All trip requests submitted through the platform, regardless of department, destination, or vehicle type.

Does NOT cover:

  • How to submit a new trip request (see MED-LOG-001)
  • Driver actions on confirmed trips (see MED-LOG-002)
  • Emergency verbal scheduling outside the platform — this must still be logged retroactively
  • Vehicle maintenance scheduling — handled under a separate maintenance SOP

04Roles & Responsibilities
RoleResponsibility in This Procedure
Logistics HandlerReviews all pending requests. Checks driver/vehicle availability. Approves, edits, or denies each request. Communicates directly with requestors when edits are made. Monitors the fleet calendar for conflicts. Escalates irresolvable conflicts to the General Manager.
RequestorSubmits trip requests via the platform. Acknowledges edited requests when notified by the Logistics Handler. Confirms or raises objections to edits within the required timeframe.
DriverPassive in this procedure — receives confirmed trip details on their calendar. No action required during review phase.
General ManagerReceives escalations for unresolvable conflicts or requests that cannot be accommodated within current fleet capacity.
05Prerequisites
  • Logistics Handler has active login access to the MEDILab internal platform
  • At least one driver is assigned and active in the system (System Active | 4 Drivers Assigned shown in the Fleet Overview header)
  • The Logistics Handler is familiar with all drivers' availability patterns and vehicle capabilities
  • The Logistics Handler has the requestor's contact information for direct communication when needed
  • Platform notifications are enabled — however, do not rely solely on notifications; check the Pending queue at least once per working day
06Procedure
PHASE AAccess the Dashboard & Identify Pending Requests
1
Navigate to the Operational Logistics dashboard. Logistics Handler
Log in to the MEDILab internal platform and go to Scheduling → Operational Logistics. The Fleet Overview header will confirm system status and the number of drivers currently assigned.

Confirm you see: ✓ System Active and the driver count (e.g., 4 Drivers Assigned). If the system shows inactive or zero drivers, do not process any requests — contact the system administrator.

2
Check the Pending (Require Approval) queue for new requests. Logistics Handler
The dashboard opens on the Pending queue by default. Each card in this queue represents a submitted request awaiting your action. Cards display: TR number, destination, client/end user, date, time window, driver assigned by the requestor, department, vehicle type, and distance classification.

Status to look for: Pending Review — this means no action has been taken yet.

If the queue is empty, no action is required. You may use the filter dropdown to also check other status views: Approved, Schedule · Denied / Requires Change · Completed, Archived.

3
Cross-reference the request date against the fleet calendar before opening the request. Logistics Handler
The calendar is displayed on the right side of the dashboard. Before clicking into any request, note the requested trip date on the calendar. This gives you immediate visual context for existing commitments on that day before you read the request details.

Use the calendar navigation arrows to move to the relevant month/week if the request is not for the current week. Days with existing trips will show dot indicators.

4
Open the trip request by clicking the card to view full Trip Details. Logistics Handler
Clicking the card opens the Trip Details modal. Review every field before taking action. Do not approve without reading all fields.

Verify the following fields are complete and logical:

  • Destination — Is it a known location? Is the trip feasible within the stated time window?
  • Date & Time — Is the time window realistic given travel distance?
  • Driver Assigned — Did the requestor pre-select a driver? Is that driver actually available?
  • Vehicle — Is the stated vehicle appropriate for the trip type?
  • Client / End User & Contact — Is a contact number provided?
  • Department & Requested By — Is this a legitimate internal request?

The modal will show four action buttons at the bottom: ✓ Approve ✗ Deny ⊘ Cancel ✎ Edit Request

PHASE BAssess Feasibility & Make a Decision
5
Assess driver availability for the requested time window. Logistics Handler
Check whether the driver named in the request is free for the full time window. A driver with an overlapping approved trip cannot be double-assigned. Use the fleet calendar to verify.

If the requestor did not pre-select a driver (field shows blank or "Any"), you must assign one. Select the driver with the lightest load on that day and no time conflicts.

Buffer rule: Allow at least 30 minutes between the end of one trip and the start of the next for the same driver, to account for return travel and handover.

6
Determine which of the three outcomes applies to this request. Logistics Handler
Every request results in exactly one of three outcomes. Do not leave a request in Pending status after reviewing it.
🔀 Decision Point — Choose One Outcome
APPROVE → All details are correct, driver is available, no conflicts. Proceed to Phase C (Step 7).
EDIT → Details have a fixable issue (wrong time, different driver needed, vehicle change). Proceed to Phase D (Step 9).
DENY → Request cannot be fulfilled: no drivers available, insufficient lead time, missing critical information that cannot be inferred. Proceed to Phase E (Step 13).
⚠️
Do not use Edit as a workaround for Deny. If a request fundamentally cannot be fulfilled (e.g., no drivers available all day), deny it with a clear reason. Editing a request implies it will be honoured — the requestor will act on that assumption.
PHASE CApprove a Request
7
Click ✓ Approve in the Trip Details modal. Logistics Handler
This action changes the request status from Pending Review to Approved and moves it to the Approved & Scheduled view. The driver's calendar is updated immediately. The requestor is notified by the system.

After approval, the trip card in the Approved view will display: TR number, destination, client, time window, driver name, vehicle, department, status Approved, and distance type.

No further communication is required from the Logistics Handler for a clean approval — the system notification is sufficient.

8
Verify the approved trip appears correctly in the Approved & Scheduled view. Logistics Handler
After clicking Approve, close the modal and confirm the card has moved from the Pending queue to the Approved & Scheduled section with status Approved. This is your confirmation the action was saved.

If the card remains in Pending after approval, refresh the page and try again. If the issue persists, contact the system administrator — do not attempt to approve the same request twice.

PHASE DEdit a Request Before Approving
ℹ️
When to use Edit: Use this path when you can fulfil the trip but the submitted details need to change — different time window, different driver, different vehicle, or a corrected destination. Editing obligates you to notify the requestor directly. They must know what changed before the trip is locked.
9
Click ✎ Edit Request in the Trip Details modal. Logistics Handler
This opens the editable form for the request. All fields submitted by the requestor are pre-filled. Modify only the fields that require correction.

Common edits:

  • Time window adjustment (e.g., 11:00–14:30 → 13:00–16:30 due to driver conflict)
  • Driver reassignment (e.g., Mohammad Abdallah → another available driver)
  • Vehicle change (e.g., Car → Van for larger cargo)

Do not change the destination or client unless you have spoken directly with the requestor first — those are core trip parameters that only the requestor can confirm.

10
Record the specific changes you are making before saving. Logistics Handler
Before saving the edit, note down (on paper or in your message draft): the original value of each field you changed, and the new value. You will need this for the mandatory notification in Step 11.

Example: "Changed driver from Mohammad Abdallah to [Name] due to scheduling conflict. Time window adjusted from 11:00–14:30 to 13:00–16:30."

11
Save the edited request and then immediately notify the requestor directly. Logistics Handler
After saving the edit and approving the trip, the system status will update — but the system does NOT automatically tell the requestor what changed. You are responsible for this communication. Use WhatsApp, phone call, or internal chat — whichever is standard at MEDILab. Do not skip this step.
📨 Required Notification — What to Communicate to the Requestor
Reference: "Your trip request TR-[XXXX] for [Destination] on [Date] has been reviewed."
What changed: State each field that was modified and the new value. Be specific — do not say "some changes were made."
Action required: "Please check the updated details in the system and confirm you are okay with these changes. If you have any objections, contact me by [time/date] so I can adjust before the trip is locked."
Contact: Provide your name and direct contact so the requestor can respond quickly.
🚨
This notification is not optional. A requestor who shows up expecting a specific driver, vehicle, or time window — and finds it changed without warning — reflects directly on logistics handling quality. It may also cause operational failures at the client site.
12
Wait for requestor acknowledgement within the agreed window; escalate if no response. Logistics Handler
Give the requestor a reasonable response window — at minimum 2 business hours for non-urgent trips, 30 minutes for same-day requests. If the requestor does not respond and the trip date is approaching, proceed with the edited version but log the attempted contact.

If the requestor objects to the changes and you cannot accommodate their preference, escalate to the General Manager before the trip date.

If the requestor confirms — no further action needed. The trip is locked and the driver proceeds as scheduled.

PHASE EDeny a Request
13
Click ✗ Deny in the Trip Details modal. Logistics Handler
Use Deny when the trip cannot be accommodated as submitted and cannot reasonably be edited to a workable state. The system will prompt for a denial reason — this is mandatory.

Valid denial reasons:

  • No drivers available on the requested date/time
  • Requested vehicle is unavailable or under maintenance
  • Insufficient lead time (same-day with no capacity)
  • Missing critical information that the Logistics Handler cannot supply (e.g., no destination specified)
  • Request falls outside business operating hours with no prior arrangement
14
Enter a clear, actionable denial reason in the system prompt. Logistics Handler
The denial reason is visible to the requestor in the system. Write it in plain language. Tell them: why the request was denied and what they should do to resubmit successfully.

Good denial reason: "No drivers available on May 27 between 11:00–14:30. Please resubmit for May 28 or later, or select an afternoon window after 15:00 on May 27."

Poor denial reason: "Not possible." — This gives the requestor nothing to act on.

15
Confirm the request status updated to Denied / Requires Change. Logistics Handler
After denial, the request moves to the Denied / Requires Change filter view. The requestor is notified by the system with your denial reason. For urgent trips, also call or message the requestor directly — do not rely solely on the system notification for time-sensitive situations.

08Non-Conformance & Exception Handling
SituationAction RequiredEscalation Path
Request submitted with less than 2 hours lead time Assess capacity immediately. If no driver is available, deny with explanation. If available, edit time window and approve — notify requestor verbally. None unless no capacity exists, then escalate to General Manager
All drivers are unavailable for a requested date Deny the request with alternative date suggestions. Do not edit to a future date without contacting the requestor first. General Manager if the trip is critical and external vehicle hire may be needed
Requestor objects to edits made by the Logistics Handler Review the objection. If the requestor's preference can be accommodated, edit again and re-notify. If not, explain the operational constraint clearly. General Manager if resolution is not reached within 1 business day
System is down and requests cannot be accessed Contact the system administrator. Do not process requests via informal channels without logging them retroactively once the system is restored. System Administrator → General Manager
A trip needs to be cancelled after approval Open the approved trip card and use the ⊘ Cancel button. Notify the driver and the requestor immediately — do not assume the system notification is enough for cancellations. None required unless pattern of late cancellations needs management review
